Help

1. Read the digest
  1. Tap a headline to jump to its brief. “Deep dive” opens more detail; “Read source” opens the original story in a new tab.
  2. Rate a headline with the three small buttons under it: 👎 not relevant, 😐 neutral, 👍 relevant. Press the same button again to clear it. Ratings, clicks and deep dives suggest keywords under “Suggested for you” in Settings.
  3. The round up-arrow button (bottom right, after you scroll) and the thick up-arrow buttons at the end of the headlines and on the right of each brief take you back to the top.
  4. Press Refresh to reload. The status line shows progress and any problem.
  5. The top bar: 🏠 Home (the digest), 🔊 Listen (when your device can read aloud), ⚙ Settings (keywords, suggested keywords, YouTube, your data) and ☰ More (Help, Feedback, About, Install app). On a phone the buttons show icons only; touch and hold or look at the page title beside Home to see where you are.
  6. On a phone the filters are folded away to save space: press Filters ▾ to open them. The line beside the buttons shows which filters are on.
2. Use the filters
  • Focus (the first row, swipe sideways; pick one or more): News & politics, Sport, Entertainment, Fun & comedy, Money, Family & kids, Health & wellbeing, Science & tech, Society & culture. Pick two or more to see stories from either. All clears it.
  • Area (second row): My Digest (only stories matching your keywords), All, a region (Local (WA/AU), US, Europe, Asia, Global), the topic areas Middle East and Iran (any outlet), or a language: English or Persian.
  • Format (under More filters): Quick reads (news items), Deep reads (long stories, analysis, opinion), Ideas & solutions, or Research (papers and reports).
  • The number beside each Focus and Format choice is how many stories you would see. A faded choice has none right now.
  • Stories are tagged by their source and by words in the title and summary, so the tags are a good guide, not perfect.
  • Deep articles on a subject: the Research format includes recent papers on topics such as social systems, parenting, mental health, inequality and housing, found through OpenAlex, an open index of scholarly work. Choose a Focus together with Research.
  • Surprise me picks a random Area, Focus and Format that has stories. Press it again for another.
  • Videos from your YouTube follow Area and Focus, and are hidden when a Format is chosen.
3. Add your own keywords (any language)
  1. Go to Settings → Keywords, type a word or phrase, such as Australian housing market or سریال ایرانی, then press Add or Enter. Separate several with commas or new lines.
  2. Every word of a phrase must appear in the story or video title, so jimmy kimmel matches more than jimmy kimmel show.
  3. Persian is matched flexibly: the Arabic and Persian forms of the letters ي/ی and ك/ک, half-spaces and accents all count as the same.
  4. Persian keywords appear right to left. Persian text works with both Persian-language stories and translated ones.
  5. Save keywords to text file puts them in the box (and downloads a file). Load from file or Load text below brings them back, for example on another device.
  6. The Try buttons add the keyword straight to your list (shown below the box) and say so; they do not fill the text box. A ✓ means it is already in your list.
  7. Choose My Digest in the Scope row to see only matching stories and videos.
4. Show videos from your own YouTube
  1. The From your YouTube panel under Headlines shows videos YouTube already recommends to you. They never leave this browser.
  2. Set up once (Chrome on a computer): in Settings press Download the Chrome add-on and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin it with the puzzle-piece icon if you like.
  3. Each time: open youtube.com (home page or search results), scroll a little so videos load, click the add-on icon and press Send. This page opens and the panel fills.
  4. Area and Focus apply to videos too. Videos have no tags, so each choice is a word rule on the title and channel name: regions and topic areas by place words, languages by script, Focus by topic words (for example “comedian” for Fun & comedy). Choosing a Format hides videos. Matches for your keywords are marked ★ and listed first.
  5. Up to 300 videos are kept. Use Clear saved videos in Settings to start over.
5. Language and audio
  • Text language (Filters panel): English, Persian or Visayan. Stories in another language are translated for you (machine translation, so check the source). Persian shows right to left.
  • 🔊 Listen (the speaker icon at the top; you can hide it in Settings → Audio) reads the top 5 or 10 headlines (after your filters) aloud with your device’s own voice. Choose headlines only or with a short brief, and pick the voice or accent in the bar that opens. Use Pause, Stop or ✕. It works without AI; if the Text language is Persian or Visayan, the headlines are translated first (needs a connection). Some devices have no Persian or Visayan voice installed: add one in the device’s speech settings. Reading may stop if the screen locks.
6. Install as an app and read offline
  1. Open the ☰ More menu (top right) and press Install app. When your browser can install the app in one tap, an install icon also appears at the top. Elsewhere the button shows the steps for your device. Once the app is installed, the install options are hidden.
  2. Android (Chrome): menu (⋮) → Install app or Add to Home screen. iPhone or iPad: open the page in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Computer (Chrome or Edge): the install icon in the address bar, or menu → Install TopicsDigester.
  3. An icon with the logo appears and the app opens full-screen. It is the same page: updates arrive by themselves when you are online.
  4. Offline: after one visit, the latest stories saved on your device open without a connection, with a note saying so. Translation and refreshing need a connection.
  5. Remove it: press and hold the icon (or use your device’s app list) and choose Uninstall or Remove. Delete all my data in Settings also removes the saved stories.
  6. The install button is hidden once the app is installed. If it does nothing on a computer, your browser may not support installing web apps.
7. Something not working?
  • Sample stories only: the news service is not reachable. Press Refresh later.
  • Nothing matches: press Reset filters, or choose a wider Scope.
  • Sent videos but the panel is empty: check the Focus and Area rows (and Format, under More filters), then press Reset filters. Choosing a Format hides videos. The add-on message says how many videos were found; if it found none, scroll YouTube further and try again.
  • The add-on says it cannot find the page: open this site once in Chrome, then press Send again. Videos arrive in the Chrome profile you sent them from.
  • Old version still showing after an update: close the app or tab completely and open it again while online; on a computer press Ctrl+F5.
  • Feedback, or a source to suggest: use the Feedback page.
  • Lost my keywords: browsers can clear site data. Export settings now and then.
8. Updates, backups and reinstalling
  • New version notice: when a new version is out, a notice appears at the top with what is new and three buttons: Update now, Back up first and Later. You can also press Check for updates on the About page.
  • Updating keeps your data. Your keywords, ratings, saved videos and settings are kept in the browser, apart from the app’s files. Update now only loads the new files; it does not touch them.
  • Installed app: it updates itself when you open it while online. If it still looks old, close it completely and open it again.
  • Before reinstalling or changing phone: go to Settings and press Export settings (this includes videos), and Save keywords to text file if you like. After reinstalling, use Import settings or Load from file.
  • Where your data lives: on Android and computers the app normally shares the browser’s storage for this site, but clearing site data, or ticking “clear data” when you uninstall, removes it. On an iPhone or iPad, the Home Screen app has its own storage, separate from Safari: keywords set in Safari do not appear in the app, and deleting the app deletes its data. Always export first.

About

TopicsDigester · Version 1.0

TopicsDigester collects news from sources you can see, groups stories about the same event, summarises them and lets you read or listen. Finite digest, no ads, nothing unsolicited, several sides, quiet by default, private by default, your language.

Summaries and translations are machine-made and may contain mistakes. Every story links to its source. News belongs to its publishers. Research and deep articles also come from OpenAlex (openalex.org), an open index of scholarly work.

Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Draft: have it reviewed before launch.

The short version. No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no tracking, no profiles. What you choose and read stays on your device. We only count totals.

What stays on your device only
  • Your keywords, relevance ratings (thumbs up, down or neutral), which stories you open, your language and audio choices, and any YouTube videos you send with the Chrome add-on.
  • They live in this browser’s own storage and are never sent to our server. We cannot see them, and nobody else can.
  • If you open the page often or install it as an app, your browser also keeps a saved copy of the page files and the latest public stories so it can open without a connection. This copy holds no information about you.
  • Use Export settings to keep a copy, or Delete all my data in Settings to erase them and the saved stories. Clearing this site’s data in your browser also erases them.
What our server holds
  • Public news only: story titles, links, summaries and cached translations. Stories are deleted after about 14 days.
  • Daily totals, kept for 90 days with no user ID: how many requests and page loads were made, how many translations were asked for, how many requests were turned away for coming too fast, and how many errors occurred.
  • Country only. To count visits by country, your internet address is looked up in a country list and the country code is counted. The address itself is not saved, not logged and not linked to anything.
  • Errors and news feeds that failed, and how much we spend on AI each day. These contain no visitor information.
What we do not collect
  • Your keywords, ratings, reading history, saved videos, name, email, exact location or device details.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising or analytics services on this page, and we do not sell or share data about you.
Technical details and other parties
  • Your internet address is held only in the server’s memory for about a minute or two so it can limit rapid repeat requests and work out your country. It is never written to our records. Our own request log is switched off.
  • Server error logs may briefly contain technical details, such as for failed connections. They are kept to a fixed size and old entries are deleted automatically.
  • Translations and summaries are made with an AI service (Anthropic). It receives the text of public news stories, never your keywords or anything about you. When you ask for a translation, your browser sends only the story numbers and the language.
  • Research papers come from OpenAlex, which our server contacts on a schedule for subjects we choose. Your device never contacts it, and your keywords are never sent to it.
  • Our hosting providers (GitHub for this page, a server host for the news service) can keep their own technical logs under their own policies, as can your internet provider.
  • When you open a source story or a YouTube video, that website’s own privacy policy applies.
  • The Chrome add-on reads the titles, channels and links of videos on the YouTube page you choose to send, passes them only to this page in your browser, and stores nothing itself.
Your choices and contact
  • Everything personal is on your device, so you control it: export it, delete it, or simply stop using the site.
  • Questions, feedback, source suggestions or takedown requests: email journey@feetontheroad.com.au, or use the Feedback page, which opens a message in your own email app. Nothing is sent from the page itself.
  • If you choose to include your keywords in a feedback email, or attach your keywords file, they are part of that email and nothing else. The page never sends them by itself.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to reply and to improve the site, and we keep them only as long as needed.
  • If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above before the change starts.
